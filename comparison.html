Linux vs Windows

A fair and balanced comparison. (By fair, we mean Linux wins in every category.)

Feature Linux Windows
Cost Free. Always. Expensive. Plus activation fees.
Privacy Respects you. No telemetry spying. Tracks everything. Every click. Every file.
Updates When you want them. "Your PC will restart in 2 minutes."
Security Built-in. Rarely gets viruses. Needs antivirus, firewall, and prayers.
Performance Lightning fast even on old hardware. Slow on the same hardware after 6 months.
Gaming Proton + Steam = thousands of games. Exclusive games that crash every patch.
Software Repositories, flatpak, snap, appimage. Random .exe files from sketchy sites.
File System ext4, Btrfs, ZFS. Reliable and fast. NTFS. Corrupts if you look at it wrong.
Support Massive community, forums, wikis. Paid support that still can't help.
Reboot Requirement Never for updates. Every single update.

Final Verdict

Linux wins. Not even close. Windows is fine for people who enjoy clicking "Next" on installers, waiting for blue screens, and paying for things Linux gives away. To each their own, but seriously, try Linux.
