Roast Windows

This page is dedicated to the operating system that thinks "restart to finish updating" is a feature, not a crime against humanity. Warning: these roasts are brutal, accurate, and completely justified.

Roast #1

The Update Nightmare

Windows updates are the only thing in life that are both unavoidable and always at the worst possible time. "Hi, we need to restart your PC to finish installing updates." No, you don't. You need to stop existing.

Roast #2

Blue Screen of Death

Linux has kernel panics. Linux has errors. Linux has logs that tell you exactly what went wrong. Windows has a blue screen that just says " :( " and expects you to be okay with that. The ":(" is the most honest part of Windows.

Roast #3

Cortana

Linux users have a terminal. Windows users have a digital assistant that nobody asked for, nobody uses, and that takes up resources while you're trying to work. At least Siri has the decency to be mocked by everyone equally.

Roast #4

Gaming? More Like Crashting?

Windows gamers act like they're superior, but their OS crashes mid-match. Linux gamers play the same games, on the same hardware, with better frame rates, and their OS doesn't die. That's not even a debate. That's a fact.

Roast #5

Privacy Theater

Windows Settings has a "Privacy" section. That's like a burglar handing you a form asking if they can look in your safe. The answer is no. Linux doesn't have a privacy settings page because it doesn't need one.

Roast #6

WSL: The Ultimate Admission

Microsoft built WSL so Windows users can run Linux inside Windows. That's the tech equivalent of buying a sports car and towing a bicycle everywhere. Just admit Linux is better. We won't tell anyone.

Honorable Mention Roasts

  • Registry edits that break everything.
  • DLL files that go missing for no reason.
  • Task Manager being the only reliable debugger.
  • "This app can't run on your PC" on a $2,000 machine.
  • Forced Microsoft account logins.
  • Telemetry that sends your data to servers you didn't know existed.
  • Bloatware you can't uninstall without PowerShell dark magic.

Conclusion

Windows isn't all bad. It's great for people who enjoy frustration, surprises, and paying money to be beta testers. For the rest of us, there's Linux. And it's beautiful.
